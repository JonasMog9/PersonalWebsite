Agent Office

A cloud-hosted team of AI agents that I run from Telegram, animated live as robots in a 2D office. A Manager routes each message to a Health Coach (recovery, training load and race predictions from my Apple Watch and Strava data) or a Market Scout that screens brand-new crypto tokens against rug-pull tests and paper-trades every alert.

  • Every score is computed in Python and covered by 300+ tests; the LLM only explains the numbers, never invents them.
  • A 54-question eval suite passes 54/54, and measuring models side by side cut the cost per answer 14×.
  • Runs on under $1 a month of API usage and replaced my CA$80/year Strava subscription.
